# BP Tracker privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

BP Tracker doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Readings** are saved to Apple Health on your iPhone, and the app reads them back from Apple Health to show the chart. Apple Health data is managed by iOS. You decide what the app can access in Health → Sharing → Apps. BP Tracker never sends health data anywhere.
- **Photos** of your monitor are read on the device to find the numbers. They aren't saved by the app, and they're never uploaded.
- **Reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Your reminder times are stored only on the device.
- **PDF reports** are created on the device. They're shared only when you choose to share them.
- There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests.

Health data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or data mining, and it's never shared with third parties.
