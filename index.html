About

I'm a lead software engineer with more than ten years of Angular behind me, going back to AngularJS. I work in industrial IoT, where the application is how engineers run fleets of edge devices on the factory floor, so clarity and reliability are very important.

I own product areas end to end: framing the customer problem, writing the PRD or ADR, agreeing the API contract with backend engineers, splitting the work into tickets, building the application, packaging the release and standing up the environment to test it. The part I enjoy most is turning a vague customer need into a design the whole team can execute, and then building it.

My depth is in frontend, and I'm at home across the rest of the stack and in system design. I work with AI coding agents every day: I break the work down, set the constraints and review every change, so they speed me up without lowering the bar. Outside work I build products of my own, most recently an iOS app on the App Store.
